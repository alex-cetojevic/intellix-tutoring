About Alex

I'm Alex — a maths graduate and former International Baccalaureate student who understands both the demands of the IB Diploma Programme and what students need to succeed in it. I graduated the IB with a 43/45, earning a 7 in Mathematics AA HL, 7 in Physics HL, and an A in my Mathematics Extended Essay. This experience allows me to guide students not only academically, but also strategically.

Over the past years, I've supported IB students across international schools throughout Europe, specialising in IB Maths AA/AI, SAT Math, Bocconi test prep, and university mathematics. My teaching style is calm, structured, and clarity-driven — I help students truly understand concepts, not just memorise methods, so they feel confident in class, during assessments, and in exams.

  • BSc in Mathematics with a very high GPA (29.3/30).
  • 500+ hours tutoring IB, SAT, and university-level maths and physics.
  • Strong track record of helping students move from low confidence and low grades to consistent, stable performance.
  • Former youth national team beach volleyball athlete — bringing discipline, focus, and consistency to tutoring.
  • A structured, patient teaching approach: clear explanations, a logical progression, and personalised guidance.
