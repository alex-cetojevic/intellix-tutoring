Teaching Philosophy

How I Teach

Most students struggling with maths aren't struggling with maths — they're struggling with gaps left by rushed explanations or memorised procedures that don't stick under pressure. My job is to find those gaps and close them properly.

Diagnose

Start with Understanding

Before diving into content, I spend time understanding exactly where the student is — what they know, what's shaky, and what the real obstacle is. No assumptions, no wasted sessions.

Build

Concepts Before Procedures

I explain the why behind every formula and method. Students who understand the logic can reconstruct answers under pressure — students who only memorise steps can't.

Apply

Practice That Actually Prepares

We work through exam-style questions systematically — building speed, accuracy and the ability to handle unfamiliar problems. Progress is tracked clearly so both student and parent can see it.
