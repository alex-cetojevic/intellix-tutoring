Full-Circle Tutoring

The lesson is just
the beginning.

Most tutors show up, explain something, and leave. At Intellix, each session is one part of a continuous cycle — so students keep improving between lessons, not just during them.

Step I

The Lesson

We work through the topic together — building understanding from first principles, not shortcuts. Every session ends with clarity on what to practise next.

Step II

The Worksheet

I prepare a custom set of practice problems — targeted at exactly what was covered. The student works through them independently, applying what they've just learned.

Step III

The Feedback

I review every answer. Misconceptions get corrected before the next session, gaps get flagged early, and nothing slips through unnoticed. Progress compounds.
