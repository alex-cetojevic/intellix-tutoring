IB tutoring · Milan

IB maths tutoring for Milan international schools.

Milan has one of the densest clusters of IB Diploma schools in Europe, and they reach the Diploma by very different routes — IGCSE at some, the MYP at others, an American curriculum or a bilingual Italian programme elsewhere. Those routes produce genuinely different strengths and gaps in maths, which is why the guides below are written per school rather than as one page.

01

What Milan students have in common

Online, not in person
All tutoring is online via Zoom or Google Meet with a shared whiteboard. That is a deliberate choice rather than a limitation: it makes scheduling around a demanding DP timetable far easier, and session notes are shared afterwards.
Three languages
Lessons run in English, Italian or Croatian. In Milan the English–Italian combination matters most: many students reason more naturally in Italian while being examined in English.
The Bocconi thread
Milan schools send a steady stream of applicants to Bocconi, whose admissions test is 24 maths questions out of 50 on a syllabus overlapping heavily with Analysis & Approaches. For students on that path the two projects should be planned together.
02

How the school routes differ

IGCSE schools
Strong procedural fluency arriving into the Diploma; the challenge is the shift from producing answers to justifying them.
IB continuum schools
Strong investigation and communication skills, which pay off in the Internal Assessment; the usual gap is algebraic speed.
American-curriculum schools
Comfortable with computation, less exposed to formal proof, and more likely to be sitting the SAT alongside the Diploma.
Bilingual and Italian schools
Mathematically strong, examined in a second language; command terms and extended-response phrasing are where marks go.
