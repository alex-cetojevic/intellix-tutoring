The rules

Clear terms, no small print.

School holidays
IB lessons run in school weeks only, so there are none in the holidays. For SAT and Bocconi, weeks are counted without school holidays.
Starting mid-semester
After the 2-week start, you pay €70 for each IB lesson left in the semester, and never more than €2,000.
Moving lessons
Lessons can move within the block, up to 4 a week before a mock or a big test.
Rescheduling
Free with 24 hours' notice. Later than that, the lesson counts as used. If I cancel a lesson, it is always made up.
Unused lessons
They expire at the end of the block. If the student is ill for a long time, we pause the block.
Paying
The 2-week start is paid before the first lesson. The rest is paid before week 3, or half before week 3 and half at the halfway point. An invoice for every payment, by bank transfer.
Stopping after the start
Stop after the 2-week start and you pay nothing more.
Not included
Exam and test fees (SAT, Bocconi), the SAT Reading and Writing section, writing any part of an IA, and lessons beyond the block's total, which are €95 each.

If anything here isn't clear, ask me. It's my job to make it clear before you pay anything.
