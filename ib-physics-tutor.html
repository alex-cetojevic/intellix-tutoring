IB Physics · SL & HL

Online IB Physics tutor, SL and HL.

I scored a 7 in Physics HL and a 7 in Mathematics AA HL, then took a maths degree. That combination matters more in Physics than in almost any other subject: most students who struggle with HL Physics are not struggling with the physics — they are struggling with the mathematics the physics is written in.

01

Why a maths graduate teaches Physics well

The real bottleneck
Fields, circular motion, simple harmonic motion and the quantum topics all break down for students at the same place: rearranging relationships, handling vectors and components, reading a gradient or an area as a physical quantity. Fix the mathematics and the physics stops feeling arbitrary.
Concept before formula
A formula booklet is provided in the exam, so memorising equations earns nothing. What earns marks is knowing which relationship applies, why, and what happens to it when one variable changes. Lessons are built around that judgement rather than recall.
Both subjects at once
Most of my students take Maths AA and Physics together. Teaching them side by side means the calculus you meet in AA gets used in kinematics the same week, instead of living in two unconnected mental boxes.
02

What we cover

The five themes
Space, time and motion · The particulate nature of matter · Wave behaviour · Fields · Nuclear and quantum physics. HL extends each of them rather than adding separate content, so HL students get depth on the same spine.
SL and HL
Sessions follow whatever your school is currently teaching, so lessons stay aligned with your class rather than running a parallel syllabus. HL-only material is treated as an extension of the SL idea it grows from.
Where marks are usually lost
Definitions stated loosely, units and significant figures dropped under time pressure, graph questions answered qualitatively when the question wanted a gradient, and extended-response questions that explain what happens without explaining why.
03

The Physics IA

The scientific investigation
The internal assessment is worth a fifth of your final grade and is the part of the course you have the most control over — it is not sat under exam conditions and it can be planned properly.
Where students go wrong
Almost always the research question: too broad, not genuinely measurable, or with no real independent variable to vary. A weak question caps the marks available no matter how good the write-up is.
What support looks like
Choosing and narrowing the question, checking the method actually tests it, working through uncertainty and error propagation, and reading drafts against the assessment criteria. The investigation stays yours — I do not write it.
04

Exam technique, Papers 1 and 2

Paper 1
Multiple choice plus data-based questions. Speed comes from recognising question types and eliminating options, not from working every problem fully. We drill that explicitly.
Paper 2
Short-answer and extended-response. Marks here follow the mark scheme’s language closely, so we practise writing answers that hit the required points rather than answers that are merely correct.
Past papers, used properly
Doing papers is not the same as learning from them. Every paper we work through gets marked, and the errors get sorted into causes — concept, mathematics, or exam technique — because each needs a different fix.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you tutor IB Physics at HL?
+
Yes. I scored a 7 in Physics HL myself and I teach both SL and HL. HL extends the same five themes rather than adding disconnected content, so HL work concentrates on depth — deriving relationships rather than quoting them, and handling the heavier mathematics in fields, circular motion and the quantum topics.
What is the difference between IB Physics SL and HL?
+
They share the same five themes; HL goes further into each and carries more teaching hours. In practice the difference students feel is mathematical: HL asks you to derive and manipulate relationships where SL asks you to apply them. That is why students who are comfortable in Maths AA usually find the HL step smaller than they expected.
Can you help with the Physics IA?
+
Yes, and it is one of the highest-value things to get help with, because the investigation is worth a fifth of your grade and is not sat under exam conditions. I help with choosing and narrowing the research question, checking the method actually answers it, uncertainty and error analysis, and reading drafts against the criteria. The work stays yours.
I am good at maths but still struggle with Physics — why?
+
Usually because Physics asks you to decide which relationship applies before you do any mathematics, and school teaching often moves straight to the formula. The fix is not more practice questions; it is building the physical picture first, then letting the mathematics follow. That is how these lessons are structured.
Do you teach the current IB Physics syllabus?
+
Yes — lessons follow the current syllabus, structured around the five themes, and always track what your school is teaching right now rather than running a separate schedule of my own.
