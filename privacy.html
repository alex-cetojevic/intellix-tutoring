Privacy

Privacy notice.

I collect as little as I can: the details you send me, and visit statistics only if you accept them. This page says what I keep, why, and for how long.

Who is responsible.

Data controller
EIGENSHIFT, obrt za informatičke usluge i poslovno savjetovanje, owner Alex Ćetojević, Mirna ulica 4, 52470 Umag, Croatia. Intellix Tutoring is the name I trade under.
Contact
For anything about your details, write to alex@intellixtutoring.com.

What I collect and why.

When you write to me
Emails and WhatsApp messages contain your name, your email address or phone number, and whatever you choose to tell me. The email box on the Bocconi drill sends me your email address and your drill result. I use these details only to reply to you and to arrange the call and the lessons. Legal basis: steps you ask for before a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
When we work together
I keep your contact details, notes on the lessons, and invoices. Legal basis: our contract and, for invoices, the law on business records (Article 6(1)(b) and (c)).
Visit statistics
Google Analytics counts visits and clicks on buttons. It runs only if you press Accept in the banner, and only then sets its cookies (_ga). You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)).
What stays in your browser
The site saves three small items on your device, not on a server: your language choice, your answers in the Bocconi drill, and your choice about statistics.
What I do not do
No newsletter, no advertising, no selling or sharing of your details for marketing, and no automated decisions.

Who else handles your details.

Formspree
Delivers messages from the email box on the Bocconi drill to my inbox.
Google
Provides my email, Google Meet for the lessons, and Google Analytics if you accept it.
WhatsApp
Only if you choose to message me there.
GitHub
Hosts this website and, like any host, sees your IP address when the pages load.
Outside the EU
Some of these companies are based in the United States. Transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or by standard contractual clauses.

How long I keep it.

Enquiries
6 months after our last contact, if no lessons follow. Then I delete them.
Students
For as long as we work together. After that I keep only what the law requires, which is the invoices.
Statistics
Google keeps the statistics for up to 14 months. The cookie lasts up to two years, unless you decline or clear it.

Your rights.

What you can ask
You can ask to see your details, correct them, delete them, limit or object to their use, or receive a copy. You can withdraw consent to statistics at any time. Write to alex@intellixtutoring.com and I will reply within one month.
Students under 18
If the student is under 18, a parent or guardian should write to me, or be copied in.
Complaints
You can complain to the Croatian data protection agency, AZOP (azop.hr). If you live in Italy, you can also go to the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).
