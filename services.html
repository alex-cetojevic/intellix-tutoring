What I Offer

Six subjects,
one tutor.

Each service is fully personalised — built around the student's current level, exam schedule, and the kind of support they actually need.

01 IB · SL & HL

IB Mathematics

Structured support for IB Maths AA and AI at both SL and HL. Syllabus mastery, past papers, exam-style questions — and real understanding built from the ground up, not just memorised steps.

02 IB · SL & HL

IB Physics

Clear, structured support for IB Physics at SL and HL. We focus on conceptual understanding, problem-solving techniques and exam strategy — so students feel prepared and confident going in.

03 SAT · Bocconi

SAT & Bocconi Test Prep

Targeted preparation for the SAT Math section and Bocconi's admissions assessment. High-yield topics, timing strategies, accuracy drills, and exam-style practice tailored to each format.

04 Summer

Summer Revision Courses

Intensive IB Maths and Physics revision courses designed for the summer break. A focused, structured programme to consolidate Year 1, close gaps, and start Year 2 with confidence.

05 1:1 · Group

Individual or Group Sessions

One-on-one lessons for fully personalised support, or small group sessions for friends and classmates covering the same topics — more affordable while remaining structured and focused.

06 Support

Resources & Briefings

Custom practice sets, revision summaries and exam questions — plus optional parent briefings to keep families informed on progress, gaps and next steps without micromanaging.
