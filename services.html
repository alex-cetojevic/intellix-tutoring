Services I Offer

Specialised online tutoring for IB Maths, SAT math, Bocconi test prep and university-level maths, plus flexible formats for individual students, small groups and parents.

IB

IB Tutoring (SL & HL)

Structured support for IB Maths and Physics at both SL and HL. We focus on understanding the syllabus, exam-style questions, past papers and clear methods so students feel prepared for the IB Diploma exams.

SAT

SAT Math Prep

Targeted SAT math tutoring with an emphasis on timing, accuracy and high-yield topics. Students receive a clear plan, focused practice, and strategies to improve scores efficiently.

Bocconi

Bocconi Test Preparation

Focused preparation for Bocconi's admissions test, with tailored practice on quantitative reasoning and problem-solving, using exam-style questions and structured review.

1:1

Individual Online Lessons

One-on-one online maths tutoring designed around the student's level and goals. Ideal for building foundations, closing gaps and developing long-term confidence in maths.

1:2

Lessons for Two Students

A collaborative option for friends or classmates working on similar topics. More affordable than individual lessons, while still structured and highly personalised.

Support

Resources & Parents' Briefings

Custom practice sets, revision summaries and exam-style questions, along with optional parent meetings to review progress, challenges and next steps.

Why IB Students Choose Me

As an IB Maths AA HL graduate and tutor, I understand both the structure of the Diploma Programme and the day-to-day reality of being an IB student at an international school. Here's what students and parents tell me they value most.

Experience

IB Insight, Not Guesswork

I've completed the IB myself with 43/45, including 7 in Maths AA HL and an A in a Maths EE. I know how internal assessments, mock exams and final exams actually feel from the inside.

Clarity

Concepts That Finally Make Sense

We focus on understanding the ideas behind formulas and methods. Students learn how to think through questions, not just copy solutions, which makes them more confident in unfamiliar problems.

Structure

A Plan for the Whole IB Journey

From Year 1 foundations to Year 2 exam revision, I help students organise their learning with clear priorities, topic checklists, and realistic study plans.

Calm

Reduced Stress, More Control

Lessons are calm, patient and honest. We slow down when needed, move faster when possible, and always keep anxiety in check so the student feels in control of their progress.

Communication

Transparent with Parents

For families who want it, I offer regular briefings on progress, weak areas and next steps. Parents stay informed without having to micromanage every detail.

Consistency

Discipline from Sport

As a former youth national team athlete, I bring consistency, reliability and a growth mindset to tutoring. We set realistic goals, track improvement, and keep showing up.
