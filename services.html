IB Mathematics

Structured support for IB Maths AA and AI at both SL and HL. Syllabus mastery, past papers, exam-style questions — and real understanding built from the ground up, not just memorised steps.

IB Physics

Clear, structured support for IB Physics at SL and HL. We focus on conceptual understanding, problem-solving techniques and exam strategy — so students feel prepared and confident going in.

Targeted preparation for the SAT Math section and Bocconi's admissions assessment. High-yield topics, timing strategies, accuracy drills, and exam-style practice tailored to each format.

The Process

Three steps to
better grades.

Getting started is simple. From your first message to your first lesson, here's exactly what happens.

01

Book a Free Call

Reach out via the form, email or WhatsApp. We'll have a 20-minute intro call to understand the student's situation, goals, and what kind of support they need.

02

Get a Tailored Plan

Based on the call, I put together a structured learning plan with clear priorities, timeline and format — designed specifically for that student's level and exam schedule.

03

Start Making Progress

Lessons begin. Each session builds on the last. Progress is tracked, the plan adapts as needed, and parents are kept informed at every stage.
