How every service works

Try it for two weeks. Then decide.

Every service runs the same way, so you always know what happens next and what you are paying for.

  1. 1

    A free 20-minute call

    We talk about where the student is now, where they need to be, and which service fits. No commitment.

  2. 2

    A paid 2-week start

    A start test, so we know exactly where the student stands. Then four lessons and worksheets with my feedback. At the end, a short call with you: what I found, the goal and the plan. It counts towards the block fee.

  3. 3

    The rest of the block, if you want to go on

    Two lessons a week until the end of the semester or the test date. A written report every 4 weeks, and a test at the end so you can see the change.

Included in every block

  • Two 60-minute lessons a week, on Google Meet
  • A worksheet after every lesson, marked before the next one
  • The student's list of personal traps, practised until they stop
  • Notes after every lesson
  • WhatsApp for short questions between lessons
  • A written parent report every 4 weeks
  • Lessons in English, Italian or Croatian

IB Maths and IB Physics

The IB year, one semester at a time.

Each semester has its own goal. One year or two years is simply these semesters in a row. More on IB tutoring, and on Milan's international schools.

One or two years
Book one semester at a time, for one year or for the whole Diploma.
School weeks
2 lessons every school week, and none in the holidays. Before you pay, we count the weeks on your school's calendar, so you know the exact number of lessons.
The IA
The IA Coach is included: topic, maths and drafts, criterion by criterion. The IA stays the student's own work. I give feedback and never write it.
Before big tests
Lessons can move within the semester, up to 4 a week before mocks and big tests, when it matters most.
Maths and Physics
Taking both? The second subject costs less, and the calculus from Maths shows up in Physics the same week.
Joining later
After the 2-week start, you pay only for the lessons left in the semester.

Extras

For one job, or a deadline that's close.

IA Coach on its own
For students who aren't in an IB block. Six lessons and my reading of two drafts, for the Maths or the Physics IA. The IA stays the student's own work: I give feedback and never write it.
Sprints
For any deadline less than six weeks away: a test, a mock, final exams, an SAT or Bocconi date that's close, or a first year university maths exam. A sprint costs more per lesson than a block, because everything has to fit into a short time.
